⏳ Election Timeline

Here is the journey of an election from start to finish.

1. Announcement

The dates for the election are declared by the Election Commission.

2. Registration

Citizens check their names on the voter list and register if missing.

3. Campaigning

Candidates travel to share their promises with the public.

4. Polling Day

Everyone goes to their local booth to cast their vote secretly.

5. Results Day

Votes are counted, and the winner is announced to the public.
