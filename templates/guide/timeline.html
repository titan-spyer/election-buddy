⏳ Election Timeline

Scroll down to follow the journey of an election from start to finish.

1. Announcement

The dates for the election are declared by the Election Commission. Model Code of Conduct comes into effect immediately.

2. Registration

Citizens check their names on the electoral rolls. New voters register themselves before the final deadline.

3. Campaigning

Candidates travel to share their manifestos, hold rallies, and debate on public platforms to win your support.

4. Polling Day

The ultimate day of democracy! Everyone goes to their local booth to cast their vote secretly using EVMs.

5. Results Day

Votes are counted securely. The winner is announced to the public and a new government is officially formed.
