⚙️ The Election Process

Think of an election like choosing a captain for a team. Everyone gets a say, and the person with the most support leads the group.

🗳️

1. Nominations

Candidates officially step forward and announce their intent to lead the people.

📢

2. Campaigns

Candidates share their ideas and promises through rallies, posters, and speeches.

☝️

3. Voting

The most important day! Citizens cast their secret vote at local polling booths.

📊

4. Counting

Votes are carefully tallied under security to ensure every single voice is heard.

🏆

5. Results

The winner is announced, and the new leader begins fulfilling their promises.
