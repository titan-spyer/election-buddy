⚙️ Process

Think of an election like choosing a captain for a team. Everyone gets a say, and the person with the most support gets to lead and make decisions for the group.

1. Nominations (Raising Hands)

First, people who want to be leaders step forward. They tell the public what they plan to do if they win.

2. Campaigns (Sharing Ideas)

The candidates travel around and talk to people. They use posters, TV, and rallies to share their promises.

3. Voting (Your Turn)

This is the most important part! On election day, you go to a booth and secretly choose your favorite candidate. Your choice is private.

4. Counting (Tallying Up)

After everyone finishes, officials count all the votes carefully. It's like adding up points in a game.

5. Results (The New Leader)

The person who gets the most votes wins! They become the new leader and start working on their promises.
