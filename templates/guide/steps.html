👣 How to Vote (Step-by-Step)

Follow these simple steps on Election Day to make your voice heard!

1

Bring ID

Take your Voter ID card or any approved government ID to the polling booth.

2

Verify Identity

Show your ID to the official. They will check your name on the voter list.

3

Get Inked

An official will put a small mark on your finger so everyone knows you voted.

4

Press the Button

Go behind the screen, find your favorite candidate's symbol, and press the blue button next to it on the EVM machine. You will hear a beep!
